The order of the evening

A simple unfolding.

Monday, 05 October 2026 · Divulapitiya Times are a gentle guide. Come unhurried — the night will wait for those we love.

  1. 8:15 in the evening

    Arrival

    Guests are received at Senuri Grand Castello. A cool drink, a quiet seat, and the first greetings of the night.

  2. 8:45

    Ceremony

    Gayashan and Vishmi exchange their vows. We ask only for your presence, and a little stillness, as two families become one.

  3. 9:15

    Photographs

    A few frames with family, and then with friends. If you are called, come smiling — it will not take long.

  4. 9:30

    Reception

    Dinner is served, and the room opens into conversation. Find your table, linger, and let the evening settle around you.

  5. 10:30

    Cake

    A pause for sweetness. The cake is cut, shared, and offered as a small thanks for every person in the room.

  6. 11:00 until late

    Dance

    The first dance, and then the floor belongs to everyone. Stay as long as the music keeps you.
