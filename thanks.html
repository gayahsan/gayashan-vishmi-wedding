With gratitude

Thank you for standing with us.

From Gayashan and Vishmi, and from both of our families.

Dear family and friends,

There are days a family prepares for in quiet, and this is one of them. With one heart we thank you — for the years that brought us here, for the road to Divulapitiya, and for every kindness already given.

On the fifth of October we gather at Centurion’s Banquet, not as two households standing apart, but as one table made ready. Whether you travel far, cross the city, send a blessing, or hold us in your thoughts from afar, your presence is the gift, and you are already part of the home that begins after the day.

We have watched two lives grow toward each other: with humour, with patience, and with a kindness that already feels like home. Thank you for blessing that beginning, and for standing with our parents as two families become one.

Come as you are. You are among family. When the photographs of the day are ready, we will share them here. Until then, please know you have our deepest thanks.

With love Gayashan & Vishmi The Perera & Fernando families
